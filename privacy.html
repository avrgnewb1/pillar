Privacy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

The short version:
  • Your data stays on your device.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • There's no tracking, analytics, advertising or third-party scripts.
  • We don't collect, receive, sell or share any of your data.

What Pillar stores, and where

The information you enter in Pillar (your profile and goals, logged meals and custom foods, workouts, weigh-ins and body measurements) is saved in your browser's localStorage on the device you're using. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server-side database, so we can't see, recover or delete it for you.

Offline files

So Pillar can work offline and be installed like an app, your browser keeps a copy of the app's own files (pages, styles, scripts and images) using a service worker. That cache holds app files only, not your personal data.

Hosting

Like any website, the server that delivers Pillar's files may keep standard technical request logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) to operate and secure the service. We don't use these logs to identify or profile you, and Pillar adds no tracking of its own.

Your control

  • Export: in the app, go to Plan → Export backup to download your data as a file.
  • Delete: use Plan → Reset all data, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage also removes it.
  • Data doesn't sync between devices or browsers. Use Export and Import to move it.

Health information

Pillar gives general fitness and nutrition estimates and is not medical advice. Because your entries stay on your device, keep your device secure if you share it with others.

Children

Pillar is intended for adults. People under 18 should use it only with guidance from a parent and a healthcare professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
