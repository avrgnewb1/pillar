FAQ

Common questions

Who is Pillar for?

People who are naturally thin, find it hard to gain weight, and want to build muscle: often called “hardgainers.” If your current app keeps nudging you to eat less, Pillar is built the other way around.

How does Pillar set my calories and protein?

It estimates your maintenance calories from your details, adds a moderate surplus for gaining, and sets a protein target based on your body weight. These are starting estimates. If your weekly average stalls, Pillar suggests a small increase.

How fast should I expect to gain?

A slow, steady gain of roughly 0.25–0.5% of body weight per week is a common target for adding muscle while limiting fat gain. Pillar tracks your trend against the pace you choose.

Do I need a gym?

The current beginner and intermediate plans are built around common gym lifts like squats, presses and rows, so the plans currently assume gym access.

What if I'm just not hungry?

That's one of the most common problems Pillar is built for. It suggests calorie-dense foods, drinks and easy add-ons sized to what you have left today, so you can hit your goal without forcing huge meals.

Is Pillar free?

Yes. Pillar is free, with no ads and no account. It runs in your browser and keeps your data on your device.
