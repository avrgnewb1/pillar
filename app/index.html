    💡 Easy calorie wins

    • Drink calories: a peanut butter banana oat shake is ~810 kcal (approx.) and goes down in minutes. Recipe in Learn.
    • Add a 4th or 5th meal: a bedtime snack of Greek yogurt + granola adds 400+ kcal.
    • Cook with fats: 1 tbsp olive oil = ~120 kcal and you barely notice it.

    Workout

    📈 Progressive overload

    • Pick a weight you can lift for the bottom of the rep range with 1–2 reps left in the tank.
    • When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add weight next time: +5 lb (2.5 kg) upper body, +10 lb (5 kg) lower body.
    • If you stall for 3 sessions, check food & sleep first, then drop the weight 10% and build back up.
    • Form first. Controlled reps, full range of motion. Muscle needs a surplus to grow, so eat!
